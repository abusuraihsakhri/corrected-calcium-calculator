Corrected Calcium Calculator

Calculate the commonly used albumin-adjusted total calcium estimate in conventional or SI units. The calculation runs entirely in your browser.

Important limitation Albumin-adjusted calcium is a historical estimate and may misclassify calcium status. It is not a measurement of ionized calcium and should not be used by itself to make treatment decisions. When accurate calcium status will change management, use directly measured ionized calcium and the reporting laboratory's reference interval.

What this tool does

  • Applies the widely used simplified albumin-adjustment equation.
  • Keeps unit systems explicit to reduce mg/dL/mmol/L and g/dL/g/L mix-ups.
  • Performs all calculation locally; values are not uploaded, stored, or logged by this page.
  • Does not estimate a treatment dose, diagnosis, or measured ionized calcium.

Evidence note

Contemporary laboratory-medicine evidence has challenged routine reporting of albumin-adjusted calcium. In settings where calcium status is clinically consequential, direct ionized-calcium measurement is preferred.
